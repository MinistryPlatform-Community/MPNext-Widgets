My Mission Trips

A participant's own mission trips with a fundraising progress bar, plus an optional donor list and — for a trip leader only — the team roster.

<next-my-mission-trips>

The team roster is trip-leaders-only, and the server enforces it

Legacy's api_MPPW_GetMyMissionTripTeamProgress has no Trip_Leader predicate at all: both of its gates (the showteamprogress attribute and a client-side tripLeader === true array filter) ran in the browser, so any signed-in participant could read every teammate's email address, mobile number and fundraising position. On a youth trip that is a roster of minors' phone numbers.

Here, show-donors and show-team-progress decide only whether the widget asks. The server answers 403 not_trip_leader to anyone whose visible pledges do not lead the trip, and the widget then hides the panel silently — a participant who is not the leader is in an ordinary state, not an error state. "Visible" is the household rule: a head of household managing a teenager's trip borrows the child's leadership, because they were already entitled to see that child's trips.

What this demo can and cannot show on the reference domain

Your trips — works, for household 1

api_MPPW_GetMyMissionTrips windows on PC.End_Date >= @DomainTime - 180. Campaign 3 ("MyChurch Test Mission Trip", End_Date 2027-12-31) is inside that window and carries pledge 2 (MPTrainer, User_Account 3) and pledge 39 (ACST: MPAdmin, User_Account 1) — both household 1. Sign in as either and you get a real trip card. Campaign 4 (End_Date 2024-05-01) is outside the window and appears for nobody.

Donors and team progress — cannot be demonstrated live here

Campaign 4 is the only campaign on this domain with a genuine Trip_Leader = true (Chris Kehayias, User_Account 98, household 85) and more than one participant (Chris, Caleb Hyatt and Sarah Kehayias in household 85, plus Mac Willingham). It is the ideal fixture for both the leader gate and the household rule — and it has aged out of the 180-day window, so its card never renders and neither panel can appear.

A reviewer who wants to see the roster panel live has to push campaign 4's End_Date forward in MP, then sign in as Chris Kehayias with show-team-progress="true". The shared reference domain is deliberately not being edited for this demo. The leader gate and the head-of-household-with-a-child-leader case are both covered in full by the fixture-driven unit tests in packages/embed-sdk/src/components/my-mission-trips.test.ts and by the service tests.

Widget CodeAdd this to any page where you want this widget

<!-- Use the widget -->
<!-- Attributes: -->
<!--   campaign-type-id="2"      REQUIRED. Pledge_Campaign_Type_ID; 2 = "Mission Project/Trip". -->
<!--   show-donors="true"        Default false. Shows who gave, with contact details the -->
<!--                             church's own unlisted flags have not suppressed. -->
<!--   show-team-progress="true" Default false. Shows the roster — but only to a trip -->
<!--                             leader; the server decides, this attribute only decides -->
<!--                             whether to ask. -->
<next-my-mission-trips campaign-type-id="2"></next-my-mission-trips>

<!-- With both optional panels -->
<next-my-mission-trips campaign-type-id="2" show-donors="true" show-team-progress="true"></next-my-mission-trips>

<!-- Listen to events -->
<script>
  const widget = document.querySelector("next-my-mission-trips");
  widget.addEventListener("myMissionTripsLoaded", (e) => console.log("Loaded:", e.detail.count));
  widget.addEventListener("myMissionTripsError", (e) => console.error("Error:", e.detail));
</script>
