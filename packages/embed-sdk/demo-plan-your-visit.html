Plan Your Visit

Two-step first-time-visitor registration: an emailed verification link unlocks the household visit-details form.

<next-plan-your-visit>
Step 1 emails a verification link back to this page (?mpp-verify-id=…). Opening that link shows the visit-details form (step 2), which creates the household + members in MinistryPlatform. Supply the template / milestone IDs configured in your MP instance below.
Sign in (optional — existing members are offered sign-in instead)

Widget CodeAdd this to any page where you want this widget

<next-plan-your-visit
  return-url="https://your-site.com/plan-your-visit"
  verification-email-template-id="1234"
  church-notification-email-template-id="1235"
  collect-address="true"
  milestone-to-assign-id="50"
  milestone-program-id="3"></next-plan-your-visit>

<script>
  const w = document.querySelector("next-plan-your-visit");
  w.addEventListener("verificationSent", (e) => console.log("Email sent:", e.detail));
  w.addEventListener("visitPlanned", (e) => console.log("Registered:", e.detail));
  w.addEventListener("loginRequired", () => console.log("Sign in"));
</script>
